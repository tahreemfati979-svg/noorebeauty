## Brand & Style
This design system captures an intimate, high-editorial cosmetic experience—bridging the cinematic glamour of legacy vanity dressing rooms with modern, warm self-expression. The interface communicates sensory luxury, tactile indulgence, and quiet confidence. Designed specifically for cross-cultural resonance, it provides effortless visual balance across both Latin LTR and Urdu RTL scripts.

The aesthetic fuses **Tactile Luxury Minimalism** with **Soft Glassmorphism**:
- Velvety, radiant surfaces that mimic skin-finish textures (dewy, satin, and soft matte).
- Champagne metallic reflections used with editorial restraint for accents and interactive micro-states.
- Generous, serene negative space that elevates cosmetic products like museum artifacts.
- Soft-focus background blooms and translucent panes that emulate filtered light through vanity mirrors.

## Colors
The palette evokes powdered pigments, whipped crèmes, warm flesh tones, and brushed vintage metalwork.

- **Primary (`#2A1E1E` — Rose Noir / Deep Espresso):** Replaces harsh standard blacks with an ultra-deep, romantic espresso-plum tone. Used for primary typography, assertive callouts, high-impact buttons, and refined brand linework.
- **Secondary (`#F5D5CE` — Petal Blush):** A soft, powdered rose. Used for primary container fills, badges, active pill states, and delicate highlight surfaces. Paired with `#FCEEE9` for ambient background glow.
- **Tertiary (`#C5A059` — Brushed Champagne Gold):** A desaturated, warm metallic accent. Reserved for micro-accents, rating stars, luxury certification seals, interactive hover glows, and hairline focus borders.
- **Neutral (`#FAF6F0` — Warm Nude Crème):** The luminous base canvas, supported by `#FDFBF7` (porcelain surface) and `#3E2B2B` (muted espresso for secondary typography and disabled states).

## Typography
The typographic architecture pairs high-fashion editorial gravitas with effortless digital clarity.

- **Headlines (Playfair Display):** Conveys dramatic elegance, reminiscent of fragrance and high-end beauty periodicals. Utilizes italic accents sparingly for emotive vocabulary (e.g., *Radiance*, *Elixir*).
- **Body & Controls (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Delivers clean legibility, generous apertures, and contemporary warmth that contrasts gently against the ornate display serif.

### Urdu / RTL Support Rules
- When rendering Urdu (e.g., Noto Nastaliq Urdu or system-level Arabic/Urdu scripts), increase line-height by a minimum factor of 1.4× over Latin equivalents to preserve ascending and descending flourishes without clipping.
- Do not apply tracking or letter-spacing greater than `0em` to cursive Arabic and Urdu typography, as kerning separation breaks orthographic letter connectors.
- Maintain visual alignment by matching baseline optical weights rather than mechanical point sizes.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to a classical 12-column fluid grid on desktop (max container width 1320px) transitioning to a 6-column grid on tablet and a 4-column grid on mobile screens. 

- **Breathing Room:** Outer margins remain wide and expansive, emulating art catalog margins.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows an 8pt base unit with occasional 4pt fractional nudges for precise pill badge vertical centering.
- **Bidirectional Reflow (LTR/RTL):** Layouts must utilize CSS logical properties (`margin-inline-start`, `padding-inline-end`, `inset-inline`) throughout. Directional indicators (e.g., chevron disclosures) automatically mirror, while non-directional media icons remain fixed.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created through gentle, diffusion-based layering rather than synthetic drop shadows:

- **Silk Shadows:** Box-shadows feature wide dispersion radii, extremely low opacity, and are tinted with warm Rose Noir (`rgba(42, 30, 30, 0.05)` to `rgba(42, 30, 30, 0.12)`) instead of neutral gray.
- **Glassmorphic Compacts:** Surfaces mimicking translucent cosmetic jars and compact mirrors use frosted fills (`rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.72)`), paired with a `16px` to `24px` backdrop blur and an ultra-fine border (`1px solid rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.22)`).
- **Surface Tiers:**
  - *Tier 0 (Canvas):* Warm Nude Crème (`#FAF6F0`).
  - *Tier 1 (Cards & Product Wells):* Porcelain White (`#FDFBF7`) with a 0.5px hairline outline (`#F5D5CE`).
  - *Tier 2 (Modals, Overlays & Drawers):* Frosted translucent veil with layered warm ambient diffusion (`0 20px 48px -12px rgba(42, 30, 30, 0.08)`).

## Shapes
The shape hierarchy emphasizes voluptuous, organic geometry derived from cosmetic compacts, lipsticks, and droplets:

- **Full Pill (`roundedness: 3`):** Applied to buttons, category pills, filter badges, and search bars to achieve an ultra-smooth, skin-friendly silhouette.
- **Micro-Containers & Product Cards:** Use soft squircle geometry (`rounded-xl` / 24px–32px radii) to frame cosmetics with luxurious gentleness.
- **Interactive Toggles & Checkboxes:** Feature circular or rounded continuous curvature without harsh right angles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Rose Noir (`#2A1E1E`) solid fill with Warm Nude (`#FAF6F0`) uppercase tracked typography (`label-md`). Fully pill-shaped with silky hover compression and a faint champagne glow (`0 0 16px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.28)`).
- **Secondary:** Transparent glass fill, hairline champagne border (`#C5A059`), and Rose Noir text.
- **Tertiary (Ghost):** Clean text with an animated champagne underline that sweeps from reading-origin (left in LTR, right in RTL).

### Chips & Filter Tags
- Delicate pill structures with `padding: 0.5rem 1.25rem`.
- Inactive state: Cream background (`#FDFBF7`) with a muted blush border (`#F5D5CE`).
- Active state: Subtle Petal Blush tint (`#F5D5CE`) with Rose Noir text and an interior micro-dot or subtle gold border ring.

### Input Fields & Selects
- Understated floating form elements with Warm Nude Crème backgrounds (`#FDFBF7`).
- Resting border: 1px subtle blush border (`#F5D5CE`).
- Focused state: 1px Brushed Champagne Gold (`#C5A059`) with an ambient gold focus diffusion.
- Error state: Muted terracotta red (`#B85C50`) with accompanying descriptive text.

### Product & Editorial Cards
- Framed in soft porcelain panels (`rounded-xl`), bordered by a hairline tint (`rgba(42, 30, 30, 0.04)`).
- Floating media container showcasing makeup swatches or bottles with generous inset padding.
- Integrated swatch selector pills beneath the image to toggle shade variations dynamically.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Radios are circular with a dual-ring target: Champagne Gold outline with a centered Rose Noir inner bead.
- Checkboxes adopt an organic rounded-sm aesthetic with smooth fade transitions upon selection.

### Beauty-Specific Elements
- **Shade Swatches:** 28px circular buttons featuring a 2px outer gap and a champagne focus rim to preview finishes (matte, dewy, shimmer).
- **Bilingual Switcher:** A minimalist pill toggle allowing instant switching between English and Urdu with smooth layout flipping across the horizontal axis.
